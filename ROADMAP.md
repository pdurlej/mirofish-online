# MiroFish Online Roadmap

MiroFish Online is moving toward a dependable synthetic research workbench, not a larger simulation for its own sake. Roadmap items are ordered by their ability to improve real publish, product, and research decisions.

## Now: Calibrated Audience Research

- [x] Stable synthetic audience runs with persona attribution
- [x] Sanitized reliability and model receipts
- [x] Independent channel-fit scoring and recommendation strength
- [x] Lexical and semantic topic similarity
- [x] Versioned topic clusters and global audience graph
- [x] Searchable, filterable research history
- [ ] Maintain a repeatable quality benchmark across models and languages
- [ ] Add export-ready research summaries for collaborators
- [ ] Explain recommendation changes between similar runs

## Next: Graph Intelligence

- [ ] Show cluster growth, momentum, and decision mix over time
- [ ] Compare two topics or branches side by side
- [ ] Improve multilingual aliases and embedding calibration
- [ ] Surface weak links and uncertain cluster membership in the UI
- [ ] Add non-destructive graph maintenance and reclustering workflows
- [ ] Make recurring objections and audience shifts visible without biasing the first persona response

## Later: Research Operations

- [ ] Saved research batches and reusable audience presets
- [ ] Controlled model bake-offs with comparable quality receipts
- [ ] Shareable read-only reports with explicit privacy boundaries
- [ ] Structured export for analysis in notebooks and other research tools
- [ ] Authentication and multi-user isolation for shared deployments

## Future Research

- [ ] Optional persona-to-persona discussion after independent first-pass reactions
- [ ] OASIS/CAMEL-style simulation for questions where social interaction is the insight
- [ ] Domain-specific audience packs and evaluation corpora
- [ ] Temporal audience models that can distinguish durable signal from trend noise

## Contribution Areas

Contributions are especially useful around evaluation datasets, multilingual similarity, graph explainability, accessibility, deployment portability, and privacy-preserving research workflows.
